Harness Club

Houston, we have a harness.

A group of builders who do harness engineering. We meet to learn it and to build it.

We start with a starter harness from the curriculum, then push the bounds. The best harness is the one you control.

The rules

  1. You do not DO talk about Harness Club.
  2. Everyone is here to learn. Ask anything.
  3. The best harness is the one you control. Build your own.
  4. No sales. No pitches. No pricing slides.
  5. Bring any model. The harness is the work.
  6. If it hasn't run, it isn't a demo.
  7. Builds go on as long as they have to.
  8. If this is your first night at Harness Club, you build.
